About the Company

Dedicated to the science of firefighting, detection & boiler technology

Mahalaxmi Engineering Pvt. Ltd. (formerly Mahalaxmi Engineering Works) was established with a vision to become one of the most recognised fire protection and boiler installation companies — through quality and development that save lives and property.

Our numerous projects span residential buildings, commercial edifices such as schools, hospitals, malls and hotels, and industrial manufacturing plants. As a provider of total fire protection systems, we undertake complete turnkey projects from design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning.

  • Strong in-house engineering & maintenance teams
  • Systems meeting Nepal Government standards
  • 24/7 support, annual & semi-annual maintenance contracts
  • After-sales service, spare parts and operator training
